Privacy Policy for Memoriq

Effective Date: October 13, 2026

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Memoriq (“we” or “our”) is a mobile application designed to help users enhance memory, focus, and cognitive skills through engaging brain games. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle data.

1. Information We Collect Automatically

To provide a functional, stable, and optimized application experience, we collect certain technical information automatically through our integration with third-party service providers:

  • Identifiers: Mobile Advertising ID (such as Android AAID or iOS IDFA), IP address, and standard unique device identifiers.
  • Device Specifications: Device model, operating system version, hardware specifications, and screen resolution.
  • Usage Data: In-app interactions, gameplay metrics, and technical interactions with mobile advertisements.
  • Location Data: General, coarse location (country and city level) derived from your IP address to provide relevant, localized context and advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected technical data for the following essential purposes:

  • App Functionality: Storing game progress, high scores, unlocked themes/skins, and app configurations locally on your device.
  • Analytics & Improvement: Analyzing generic performance data to fix bugs, optimize frame rates, and refine memory game challenges.
  • Advertising Operations: Serving relevant advertisements that support the ongoing free availability and updates of the application.
  • Legal Compliance: Fulfilling strict global privacy obligations, including passing regional consent signals to ad networks.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

Memoriq integrates third-party advertising to keep the application accessible to all users. We use Google AdMob as our main ad delivery infrastructure. Google AdMob may collect and process your Advertising ID and IP address to measure ad performance, detect fraud, and serve advertisements.

You can review the privacy operations of our partner network here:

  • Google AdMob & Partner Policies: Privacy Policy

4. GDPR & International Privacy Rights (EEA/UK/Brazil)

If you reside within the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, processing your personal data relies on the explicit Consent you grant via the app's integrated Consent Management Platform (CMP).

  • Consent Management: You maintain the right to adjust, withdraw, or update your privacy preferences at any time through the "Settings" or "Privacy" interface inside the application.
  • Data Rights: You hold the legal right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of any personal data handled by us or our integrated services.

5. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have specific regulatory protections concerning the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data. While we never sell user data for financial profit, sharing device-level Advertising IDs with advertising networks falls under the legal definition of a data "sale/share" in California. You can opt-out of behavioral tracking through your device-level operating system controls (e.g., "Limit Ad Tracking" on Android or "Ask App not to Track" on iOS).

6. Children’s Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Memoriq is built with a strong focus on user safety and complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global minor safety frameworks.

When the application identifies that a user is under the legal age of digital consent in their country, we configure the ad SDKs to request **Non-Personalized Ads only**, completely disabling interest-based tracking, profiling, and targeted behavioral advertising. No personal tracking data is knowingly extracted from minors.

7. Data Retention & Local Storage

Your cognitive progress, settings, unlocked skins, and high scores are stored strictly locally on your physical device using native system storage utilities (**Hive** / **SharedPreferences** on Android and local storage keys on iOS). This user data remains completely isolated inside your mobile operating system and is permanently wiped automatically when you uninstall the application. We do not maintain off-device cloud servers for your personal gameplay progress.
